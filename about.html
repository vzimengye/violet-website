About Me

I study Computer Science and Economics at UC Berkeley. I grew up in China, was born in Japan, and now live in Berkeley.

Most of my work right now is machine learning and data engineering. I like projects where the model is only part of the job and you also have to clean the data, check whether the results hold up, and ship something people can use.

Before CS, I spent a lot of time on math competitions and on ESG and green finance research, which is how I ended up modeling electric bus fleets in high school.

Outside class I take photos, ski, travel, draw, and help produce shows with the Chinese theater group.
